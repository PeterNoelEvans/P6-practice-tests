Micekings Appreciation

The Micekings Universe

The Micekings series by Geronimo Stilton takes readers on fun, adventurous journeys featuring Viking-like mice living in a cold and rugged land. The stories include brave characters, comical challenges, and lessons about teamwork and courage. Even if you’ve read only one or two of these stories, you can still appreciate the main characters, the typical story structure (with a beginning that sets the scene, a middle that introduces challenges, and an end that resolves them), and the imaginative world of the Micekings.
