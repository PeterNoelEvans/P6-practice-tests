Grammar Section

Stage 1: Basic Grammar

1. Read the short paragraph and choose the correct answer:

"Lisa wakes up early every morning. She makes breakfast for her little brother and then walks him to school before going to her own classes. She always says, 'Breakfast is the most important meal of the day!'"

Which sentence about Lisa is correct?

a. Lisa wakes up late.
b. Lisa has no brother.
c. Lisa walks her brother to school.
d. Lisa hates breakfast.

2. Read the conversation and choose the best response:

Mother: "Have you finished your homework, Kevin?"
Kevin: "Not yet, Mom. I'm still working on it."

What does Kevin mean?

a. He finished his homework yesterday.
b. He hasn't started his homework.
c. He is still doing his homework.
d. He doesn't have any homework.

3. Read the text:

"My family goes to the park every Sunday. We pack a picnic basket, play games, and enjoy the sunshine. It's our favorite day of the week!"

Which sentence is true according to the text?

a. The family goes to the park every day.
b. They do not like the sunshine.
c. They bring food to the park.
d. They always go on Saturday.

4. Read the short note:

"Sara is going to the store to buy milk and eggs. She hopes to be back home before noon."

Which is correct about Sara?

a. She wants to buy flowers.
b. She plans to buy milk.
c. She went to the store yesterday.
d. She doesn't plan on returning home.

5. Read the dialogue:

Teacher: "Where is your homework, John?"
John: "I'm sorry, I left it at home."
Teacher: "Remember to bring it tomorrow."

What can we infer about John?

a. He completed his homework but forgot it.
b. He didn't do his homework.
c. He never does his homework.
d. He said he didn't have homework.

c. cooking
d. cooked
