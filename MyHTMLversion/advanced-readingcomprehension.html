The Floating Lantern Festival

In Thailand, the Floating Lantern Festival, also known as Loy Krathong, is celebrated every year on the night of the full moon in November. During the festival, people gather by rivers and lakes to release beautifully decorated lanterns, or krathongs, onto the water. Made from banana leaves, flowers, and candles, the krathongs are a way to honor the water goddess and ask for good fortune. The reflection of the lanterns on the water creates a magical sight.

Families and friends often make their own krathongs at home, decorating them with intricate patterns. At night, the atmosphere is lively with music, dancing, and fireworks. For many, Loy Krathong is not just about traditions; it is also a time to reflect on the past year and let go of negative feelings. Tourists from all over the world visit Thailand to witness this unique festival, adding to the excitement.
